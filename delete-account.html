Delete your account

What's That Meme? · by Siddharth Gholap

Delete it in the app (immediate)

  1. Open What's That Meme? and sign in.
  2. Go to the Profile tab.
  3. Scroll to Settings and tap Delete online account.
  4. Confirm. Your account and online data are deleted straight away.

Or ask us by email

Email weasmr314@gmail.com from the email address you signed in with, with the subject "Delete my account". We delete it within 7 days and reply to confirm.

What gets deleted

  • Your account (sign-in email and the name from Google)
  • Your username, avatar and country
  • All your online games and scores: Daily Challenge, ranked runs, 1v1 and friend games, and your places on leaderboards
  • Your friends, friend requests, game invites and rewarded-ad confirmations

What's kept

  • Nothing is kept on our server after deletion. Copies in routine server logs expire automatically within 30 days.
  • Scores and settings stored only on your phone (Pass & Play, Beat the Clock) are removed when you uninstall the app, or with Profile → Reset scores.
  • Data Google collects for ads is handled under Google's privacy policy.

See also our privacy policy.
