Privacy Policy

What's That Meme? · Effective 6 October 2026

What's That Meme? ("the app") is a meme guessing game made by Siddharth Gholap ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and how you can delete it.

What stays on your phone

Pass & Play and Beat the Clock work offline. Their scores, personal bests, the player names you type, and your settings (sound, haptics) are stored only on your device. We never receive them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

What we collect when you sign in

The app asks you to sign in with Google (or email) so your online scores, ranks and friends stay with you. We collect:

DataWhy
Email address and the name on your Google accountTo create and secure your account. Your name is only used to suggest a username; it isn't shown to other players.
Your username and emoji avatarShown to other players on leaderboards, in 1v1 and in friend games.
Your countryTaken from your phone's region setting (not GPS) for the India / country leaderboards. Shown as a flag next to your username.
Online game data: your answers, scores and when you played (Daily Challenge, ranked runs, 1v1 and friend games)To run the games, score them fairly on our server, and rank them.
Friends, friend requests, game invites, and when you last had the friends screen openTo let you play with friends and show them when you're around.
Rewarded-ad confirmations: which ad views earned you a Daily Challenge retry or kept your streakSo these rewards are fair on the leaderboard: each ad view can only be used once.

During a live game, your progress (how many memes you've answered and your score) is sent to the other players in that game as it happens. It isn't stored.

Ads

The app is free and shows ads from Google AdMob. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, Google may collect your device's advertising ID, IP address (which gives an approximate location), device information and how you interact with ads. See how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

  • In the EU, UK and Switzerland, the app asks for your consent with Google's consent form before any personalised ads. You can change your choice any time in Profile → Ad privacy choices.
  • On iPhone, the app asks for permission to track (Apple's App Tracking Transparency). If you say no, you still get ads, just not personalised ones.
  • Ads are limited to content suitable for teens.
  • Rewarded ads are always your choice. For rewards that affect online ranks (a Daily Challenge retry, keeping your streak), Google confirms to our server that the ad was watched, along with your account ID, so the reward can be given to you.

Who we share data with

We don't sell your data. We only use these service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase: hosts our server and database (stored in Mumbai, India) and handles sign-in.
  • Google: Google Sign-In, and AdMob for ads.
  • Expo: delivers app updates. When the app checks for an update it sends basic information such as the app version and platform.

Other players can see your username, avatar, country flag and game scores. Your friends can also see when you're hosting a game or were recently active.

How long we keep it, and deleting it

We keep your online data until you delete your account (how to delete your account). To delete it, open Profile → Delete online account in the app. This immediately and permanently deletes your account, username, scores, ranks, friends and game history from our server. You can also email us to ask for deletion. Data on your phone is removed when you uninstall the app or use Profile → Reset scores.

Security

Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS). Scores are checked on our server, and the database can't be read directly from the app.

Children

The app is meant for people aged 13 and over and isn't directed at children. Some memes contain mild swearing and crude humour. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we'll delete it.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act or the EU/UK GDPR), you can ask to access, correct or delete your data, or withdraw consent. Most of this you can do in the app; for anything else, email us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update this page and the date above, and tell you in the app for important changes.
